Wikidata LexiGuess

Guess the word using lexicographical data from Wikidata

How to Play Wikidata LexiGuess

1. Select your preferred language and difficulty level.

2. A word from Wikidata will be chosen based on your language and difficulty settings.

3. Guess the word by typing it in the input box and clicking "Submit Guess".

4. Alternatively, guess letters one by one. Correct letters will appear in the word display.

5. Use the hint buttons to reveal helpful information or letters. Each hint costs points!

6. You have a limited number of lives. Guess correctly before you run out of lives!
